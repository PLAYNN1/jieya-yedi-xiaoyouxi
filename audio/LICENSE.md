# Audio rights

The WAV recordings in this directory are separate from the MIT-licensed code.
They are processed excerpts of a reference recording provided by the project
maintainer. No ownership of the original sound recording is asserted.

The project maintainer confirmed permission to redistribute these recordings
on 2026-10-08. This records permission to include the audio in the project;
it does not assert that the recordings are public domain or MIT licensed.
No standalone sublicensing terms are granted by the software license.

The maintainer authorized inclusion of these excerpts and processed sound
effects in the public game repository. The complete original recording is
not included.

The audio-processing code in `tools/make-audio.js` is covered by the repository's
MIT license. Its license does not grant rights to input recordings or samples.
